AI, external services & integrations

What the site leans on

The server runs no AI and pays for no AI service. The only language model is an opt-in debrief in PoCapitol that runs on the player's own machine or inside their browser. Everything else outside the app is free, read-only, and has a fallback.

AI and models

In plain wordsThe computer opponents are hand-written rules, the quiz questions come from a fixed list, and the voices were recorded once ahead of time. Nothing is "generated" while you play unless you switch it on yourself in PoCapitol.
