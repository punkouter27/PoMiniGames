Visual and sensory roadmap - 20 ideas

Adaptive cinematic render budget

Dynamically scale temporal resolution, particle count, shadow distance, AO, and post effects from measured frame time while keeping the selected graphics mode as the maximum quality.

Graphics

One-second pre-impact light bloom

Add localized bloom, rim-light pulse, and exposure dip before major impacts, wins, knockouts, and race overtakes so players read the event before the sound arrives.

Best fitPoBrawl, Voxel Strike, PoCabinet
ImplementationShared feel presets plus event-timed post-process parameters

GraphicsPhysics

Deferred damage material signatures

Display impact heat, dust, scorch, chipped paint, dents, cracks, and wetness at the exact collision point instead of using a single generic debris burst.

Graphics

Ray-marched environmental atmosphere

Render wisps, smoke, fog, and gas as animated low-resolution volumetric fields that are composited over the 3D scene and respond to wind and explosions.

Best fitPoCapitol, PoVoxelStrike, PoEcosystem

GPU particle and decal pools

Move recurring bursts, trails, tire spray, sand, sparks, rain, and muzzle dust into fixed GPU pools with instanced rendering and no per-particle allocation spikes.

Phase 1 - Shared foundation

Centralize impact presets, GPU pools, adaptive quality, audio scene routing, spring transitions, and reduced-motion behavior.

Phase 2 - Signature moments

Upgrade one knockout, one race collision, one menu transition, and one result ceremony for immediate platform-wide polish.

Phase 3 - Advanced scenes

Add ray-marched atmosphere, soft objects, reverb zones, adaptive stems, and high-quality debris to flagship games only.
