PoFighter shot list

The 10 moves one performer films to become a PoFighter character. Tick each box on set. The name in grey after each move is what goes in <id>.moves.json (see scripts/pofighter/README.md).

Before you roll

  • Phone fixed on a tripod, landscape, at hip height, 1080p or better (4K gives a sharper fighter). Don't move or zoom it during the session.
  • Whole body in frame for every move, with room above the head (jumps) and a strip of floor under the feet.
  • Side-on, always facing the same way (say, toward the left edge of the frame). The packer mirrors one direction; a turn mid-session breaks it.
  • Same distance from the phone for every move. Mark the spot on the floor with tape. A move filmed closer shows up bigger in the game.
  • Plain background, nobody else in shot. Clear bags, chairs and blankets; clothes that stand out from the wall; even light, no window behind.
  • A mat for the falls.
  • Between moves, hold the fighting stance for one second, so every move starts and ends in the same pose.
  • Do each move three times; the best take is picked later. One long video is fine.

The 10 moves must

Every attack: snap it out, hold the extended limb for a beat, return to stance.

  1. Fighting stance idle

    Guard up, bounce lightly on the spot for 3 seconds.

    The pose every other move starts and ends in.

  2. Walk forward walkF

    5 steps forward in guard, at an even pace.

    The game measures the step length so the feet don't slide.

  3. Walk back walkB

    5 steps backward in guard.

    Walking back is the standing block; if skipped, the forward walk is played in reverse.

  4. Crouch crouch

    Drop low into a crouching guard and hold it for 2 seconds.

    Ducks punches, the low block, and the pose a jump is drawn in if no jump is filmed.

  5. Jump jump

    Straight up, knees tucked, land back in stance.

    Needed for jump-ins; Kim has none yet.

  6. Punch punchMid

    A straight punch at chest height toward the foe's side.

    One of the two attacks; ducked by a crouch.

  7. Kick kick

    A front or side kick at waist height, leg fully out.

    The other attack: slower, harder, reaches farther.

  8. Victory cheer victory

    Arms up, celebrate on the spot for 3 seconds. Stay on the tape mark: don't walk at the phone.

    Plays after every round won.

  9. Knockout fall ko

    Get hit, fall backward onto the mat, and lie still for 2 seconds.

    K.O.s and every knockdown (a hit in the air).

  10. Get up getUp

    From lying down, get up and finish in stance.

    Plays after every knockdown.

Voice and lines must

Recorded close to the phone (or a mic) after the moves, in a quiet room. One clip per cell; a short shout or phrase is enough. Files go to wwwroot/audio/pofighter/<id>/<mood>-<level>.wav, then run scripts/pofighter/voices.ps1.
