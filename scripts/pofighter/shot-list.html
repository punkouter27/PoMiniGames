PoFighter shot list

Everything one performer records so they become a full PoFighter character. Tick each box on set. The name in grey after each move is what goes in <id>.moves.json (see scripts/pofighter/README.md).

Before you roll

  • Phone fixed on a tripod, landscape, at hip height, 1080p or better (4K gives a sharper fighter). Don't move or zoom it during the session.
  • Whole body in frame for every move, with room above the head (jumps) and a strip of floor under the feet.
  • Side-on, always facing the same way (say, toward the left edge of the frame). The packer mirrors one direction; a turn mid-session breaks it.
  • Same distance from the phone for every move. Mark the spot on the floor with tape. A move filmed closer shows up bigger in the game.
  • Plain background, nobody else in shot. Clear bags, chairs and blankets; clothes that stand out from the wall; even light, no window behind.
  • A mat for the falls.
  • Between moves, hold the fighting stance for one second, so every move starts and ends in the same pose.
  • Do each move three times; the best take is picked later. One long video is fine.

Required moves must

Without these the fighter can't play. Every attack: snap it out, hold the extended limb for a beat, return to stance.

  1. Fighting stance idle

    Guard up, bounce lightly on the spot for 3 seconds.

    The pose every other move starts and ends in.

  2. Walk forward walkF

    5 steps forward in guard, at an even pace.

    The game measures the step length so the feet don't slide.

  3. Walk back walkB

    5 steps backward in guard.

    Walking back is the standing block; if skipped, the forward walk is played in reverse.

  4. Crouch crouch

    Drop low into a crouching guard and hold it for 2 seconds.

    Ducks punches, the low block, and the pose a jump is drawn in if no jump is filmed.

  5. Jump jump

    Straight up, knees tucked, land back in stance.

    Needed for jump-ins; Kim has none yet.

  6. Punch punchMid

    A straight punch at chest height toward the foe's side.

    Also the base for the jump punch and, until filmed, the specials.

  7. Kick kick

    A front or side kick at waist height, leg fully out.

    Also the base for the jump kick.

  8. Victory cheer victory

    Arms up, celebrate on the spot for 3 seconds. Stay on the tape mark: don't walk at the phone.

    Plays after every round won.

  9. Knockout fall ko

    Get hit, fall backward onto the mat, and lie still for 2 seconds.

    K.O.s and every knockdown (sweeps, Rising Fist, hits in the air).

  10. Get up getUp

    From lying down, get up and finish in stance.

    Plays after every knockdown.

Signature move one per fighter

Every fighter gets one special move of their own, invented by the performer on the day. Anything that reads as a single strong attack works: a spinning backfist, a flying knee, a two-handed push, a throw-like "energy" gesture. It's discovered in the recording: whatever they do becomes that fighter's signature.

  • Keep it 1 to 3 seconds, starting and ending in stance, staying roughly on the tape mark.
  • Ask the performer for a name (shown on screen as it fires, e.g. "THUNDER KNEE").
  • Decide its kind: strike (hits where they stand), rush (travels forward into the foe), rise (leaves the floor, beats jumpers) or projectile (a throwing gesture; the game adds the energy ball).
  • Decide its button: punch or kick. Every signature is input as ← ↙ ↓ ↘ → + that button (half circle toward the foe).
  • A fighter without a filmed signature has none, so it's fine to add one in a later session.

  1. Signature move signature

    Performer's own move, three takes. Write down: name ______________ · kind ________ · button ________

    Packed like any attack; the name, kind and button go in the moves file (below).

Recommended moves better

The game works without these. Each one is used automatically once it's in the fighter's data; until then the move borrows the punch or kick (or, for the crouch attacks, doesn't exist).

  1. Crouch jab crouchPunch

    From the crouch, a quick punch forward, staying low the whole time.

    Down + punch. Not offered at all until filmed: sprites are never shrunk to fake a crouch.

  2. Leg sweep sweep

    From the crouch, sweep one leg out along the floor and back.

    Down + kick: the low attack that knocks down. Not offered until filmed.

  3. Jump kick jumpKick

    Jump forward and kick at the top of the jump, land in stance.

    Kick in the air. Until filmed, the standing kick rides the jump arc.

  4. Jump punch jumpPunch

    Jump forward and punch downward at the top, land in stance.

    Punch in the air.

  5. Po Blast throw blast

    Both palms thrust forward from the hip, hold for a beat.

    ↓ ↘ → + punch. The energy ball is drawn by the game.

  6. Rising Fist risingPunch

    A jumping uppercut: crouch, spring up with the fist overhead, land in stance.

    → ↓ ↘ + punch, the anti-air.

  7. Rush Kick rushKick

    Two quick steps forward into a kick. Start on the tape mark; it's fine to travel.

    ↓ ↙ ← + kick.

For later future

Not used by the game yet. They're cheap to film while the set is up and would let a future version add them.

  1. Standing block blockHigh

    Arms up in a tight guard, flinch as if a hit lands on it.

    A block pose instead of walking back.

  2. Crouching block blockLow

    Same, from the crouch.

  3. Hit reaction hurt

    Head snaps back from a punch to the face, then recover to stance.

    A real flinch instead of the white flash.

  4. Throw throw

    Grab forward and toss an imaginary opponent over the shoulder.

    A throw system.

  5. Taunt taunt

    A short show-off gesture at the foe.

Voice and lines must

Recorded close to the phone (or a mic) after the moves, in a quiet room. One clip per cell; a short shout or phrase is enough. Files go to wwwroot/audio/pofighter/<id>/<mood>-<level>.wav, then run scripts/pofighter/voices.ps1.

happyLanding hits and combos"heh" · "nice" · "yes!" · "got you!" · laugh

  • The signature's name, shouted once as if throwing it.
  • Three win quotes: short lines to the beaten foe (one sentence each). They go into wwwroot/data/pofighter/quotes.json; "{foe}" stands for the loser's name.

After the shoot: the moves file

Frame ranges come from the contact sheets as before. The signature is one more move plus a block that names it; pack.py copies the block into the fighter's data.

{
  "fighter": "newid",
  ...
  "moves": {
    "idle":      {"from": 10, "to": 50, "step": 2, "anchor": "world", "loop": "pingpong"},
    ...
    "signature": {"from": 900, "to": 940, "step": 2, "anchor": "world", "loop": "once", "attack": true, "advance": true}
  },
  "signature": {"name": "Thunder Knee", "button": "kick", "kind": "rush", "damage": 18}
}

kind: strike, rush, rise or projectile. damage is optional (5–30, default 18). A rise filmed in the air is drawn from the footage, not lifted twice.
