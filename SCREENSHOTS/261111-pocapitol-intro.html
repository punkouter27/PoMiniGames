PoCapitol: Opening Establishing Shot

A five-second crane and pan above the west front when an attempt opens, so the whole scene is in view before the player is put behind the crowd. Annotated capture: SCREENSHOTS/261111-pocapitol-intro.png.

Opening camera

Before
The scene opened on the third-person camera already behind the player on the lawn: the Capitol in the distance, no sense of the grounds, the crowd or the scale of the building.
After
Five seconds high over the lawn: the camera cranes up from behind the player and sweeps from the south-west round to the north-east, holding the west front in frame — dome and drum, both wings, the arch, the inaugural stands, the police lines, the fencing and the crowd. Then it eases back down behind the player over 1.6 seconds.

How it behaves

  • Runs once per attempt, armed from start() in index.js — so it plays when the intro card closes, not behind the dimmed card.
  • The world is held still for the five seconds: no advance, so the clock, the crowd and the lines do not move before the player can act.
  • Crane in and out are eased blends of both position and aim, so there is no cut at either end.
  • Haze is cut by 40% while the shot runs, or the whole grounds would sit in fog.
  • Skipped entirely when prefers-reduced-motion is set or the app asks for less flashing.
  • No new dependency, markup, configuration or DOM: the whole shot lives in scene.js and is driven by the existing frame loop.
