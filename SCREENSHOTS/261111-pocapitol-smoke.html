PoCapitol: Gunsmoke Lingers, and the Crowd Steps Over the Fallen

Annotated capture: SCREENSHOTS/261111-pocapitol-smoke.png; the pile capture is SCREENSHOTS/261111-pocapitol-bodies.png.

1. Muzzle smoke, four times as long

Before
puff(...) for a gunshot lived 0.9–2.3 s with drag 2.2 and alpha up to 0.30. In a firefight it was gone before the next round, so nothing accumulated and no haze ever formed.
After
puff takes a life, alpha and drag; a gunshot now uses life: 4, so it lives 3.6–9.2 s — exactly four times. Alpha is eased to 0.8 and drag to 1.5, so each puff is thinner but slower and hangs, and a sustained firefight builds a grey haze over the line instead of a series of blinks.

2. The crowd walks over the fallen

Before
Anyone with zero health was still a full obstacle: pushed into the steering body list and separation-tested at 0.68 m with four times the mass. A heap of casualties became a wall, and the crowd behind it could not pass.
After
In the armed demo the fallen are neither steering obstacles nor separation partners, so survivors walk onto and over them and keep advancing. Because people can now stand on the heap, someone shot there dies on top of it and the resting solver stacks them a layer higher — the pile grows rather than the queue.

Verification
