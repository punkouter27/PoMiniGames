1. The crowd doubled

Before
CROWD_SIZE = 256, spawned in a 32-wide grid eight rows deep.
After
CROWD_SIZE = 512. Doubling the grid rows alone pushed the back rank to z = 116, past the navigation grid's edge at 109, and the renderer threw “NPC left the scenario navigation bounds” on the first step. The formation is now 36 across by 15 rows (2.05 m by 1.9 m), so all 512 start on the grass inside x −37…37, z −60…109.

2. Nobody gets stuck on the pile

Rule
In the armed demo a body at zero health is neither a steering obstacle nor a separation partner, so a heap of 268 casualties is something people walk onto and over. With twice the crowd, that is what keeps the column moving: the demo still breaks all five lines in order and reaches the chamber doors before the curfew.
Evidence
The right-hand capture is 95 s in with 268 down. The left is 45 s in with 175 down, taken from above the crowd: living raiders are standing among and over the bodies and are already well up the plaza.

3. The bike racks are objects now

Before
The racks were decoration drawn from the line's integrity: they bowed, but nothing could move them and they changed nothing.
After
Nine racks live in the simulation. Each takes the crowd's weight in its own slot, leans, rocks, and goes over once it has been leaned on for six raider-seconds, then is dragged aside. Where a rack is down the crowd may press 0.8 m closer, right up to the officers — so shoving a rack over genuinely increases the pressure on the line and tires it faster. Losing a rack is not losing the line: the officers still hold.
