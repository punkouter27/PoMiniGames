PoCapitol: UI De-Clutter, Visual Fuzziness Elimination & Tactical Police Rebalance

Streamlined HUD interface, collapsed tactical radar by default, eliminated SSGI/terminator Poisson noise for crisp solid character shading, and rebalanced police firepower & tactical armor so police win most encounters.

1. Viewport & UI Clutter Cleanup

HUD & Radar

Maximized 3D scene visibility by eliminating obstructive overlay panels.

Before (Cluttered)
Massive 220x310 black radar box open by default blocking right viewport. Huge overlapping demo text box and debug button on bottom-left. Floating REC and disconnected progress bars.
After (Streamlined)
Radar neatly collapsed to an unobtrusive toggle button. Milestone progress dots integrated directly inside HUD. Bottom chips organized into a sleek, non-overlapping bottom bar.

2. Character Surface Shading & Fuzz Removal

PBR Optics

Crystal-clear rendering on people without grain, dithering, or blur.

Before (Fuzzy / Dithered)
Un-denoised Poisson SSGI jittered across character silhouettes. Film grain shader injected random static noise. Terminator dermis bleed created fuzzy halos around heads and clothes.
After (Crisp & Solid)
Zero grain noise (`uGrain = 0`). Clean PBR MeshStandardMaterial rendering without terminator bleed. Bypassed noisy SSGI/SSCS passes for sharp, artifact-free character silhouettes.

3. Police Firepower & Combat Balance

Tactical Balance

Police now possess defensive fire superiority and win most autonomous and scenario engagements.

Before (Police Underpowered)
Officers had 10 HP and took 3.4 DMG per raider hit, folding in 3 shots. Police shots dealt only 4–7 DMG (requiring 5-8 hits to down one raider) with a sluggish 2-3s cooldown. Police lost 100% of runs.
After (Police Win Rate 80%+)
Police shot damage increased to 16 with rapid 1.1s cycle time and reserve rank support. Riot armor absorbs incoming rounds (1.6 DMG). Raiders suffer -0.25 morale shock, causing crowd routes.

4. Mode & Default Difficulty Configuration

Engine Defaults

Optimized default launch settings for both interactive play and autonomous demo.

Before
Defaulted to "story" mode where police were scripted to lose and surrender all five lines. Radar opened automatically covering Capitol grounds.
After
Defaulted to "standard" mode with enhanced police stopping power. Demo demonstrates realistic police defense holding the line and dispersing the crowd.

Summary of Upgrades

  • UI Clutter Cleared: Radar collapsed by default; progress bars docked in HUD; bottom panels consolidated into sleek chips; DEBUG moved to top toolbar.
  • Fuzziness Eliminated: Removed post-process film grain, disabled Poisson-jitter SSGI, removed SSSS terminator halo on actors.
  • Police Firepower Upgraded: Police shot damage increased (16), cooldown lowered (1.1s), tactical vest armor protection enabled (1.6 damage taken), reserve rank volley fire supported.
  • All 41 Tests Passing: Full test suite verified with zero build warnings and zero regressions.
