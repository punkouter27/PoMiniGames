PoCapitol: Battlescene and Capitol Scenery Accuracy

The west front and the three interiors are redrawn against the Architect of the Capitol and House history references the PRD cites. Playable footprint, collisions and objectives are unchanged: this is decoration in architecture.js, plus the Statue of Freedom's drum in scene.js and two-force police dress in crowd.js. Annotated before/after captures: SCREENSHOTS/261111-pocapitol-accuracy.png.

1. West front terrace, stairs and the doorway

Exterior
Before
Two flat step blocks, no parapets, an open wall between the portico columns, and a plain empty plaza.
After
Raking marble cheeks with balusters climb beside both flights, newel piers carry bronze standards, and a sandstone elliptical arch with voussoirs and a gilt keystone frames the Lower West Terrace doorway. Bronze lamp standards flank the steps; bunting hangs on the rails.

2. The grounds as they were dressed

Exterior
Before
Two inaugural stands on a bare lawn; nothing marking the perimeter.
After
Green snow fencing runs down both lawn edges with fallen panels, "AREA CLOSED" boards stand on the barricade line, and a scaffold press tower with two decks and a camera stands out on the west lawn.

3. Two forces on the line

Battlescene
Before
Every officer in the same dark navy coat and helmet.
After
Capitol Police stay dark navy; about two in five are Metropolitan Police in hi-vis yellow-green outer jackets with reflective bands. Same helmets, shields, batons and spray as before.

4. Rotunda

Interior
Before
Plain sandstone walls, one flat brown panel per painting position, ring floor, coffered dome.
After
Eight arched gilt frames with painted canvases, olive wreaths in the frieze over each pilaster, a proper cornice, the Frieze of American History in the belt under the windows, the Apotheosis painted in the canopy around the oculus, and a brass ring round the white marble centre of the floor.

5. Crypt

Interior
Before
Six squat columns, a grey floor, a two-tone compass disc.
After
Rows of Aquia Creek sandstone Doric columns against both walls, Seneca paving with visible joints and wear, wall niches holding draped figures for the original colonies, and an eight-point white marble compass stone with a brass centre.

6. Statuary Hall

Interior
Before
A rectangular room: white columns, scattered dark mats on the floor, six cone statues.
After
Variegated Breccia marble columns under Carrara capitals, a full black and white marble floor, the cast-steel ceiling lantern, the Car of History clock over the Rotunda door, the sandstone relief eagle in the frieze, and more of the collection on plinths in wall niches.

7. Dome and drum

Silhouette
Before
The Rotunda's inner dome rose to 19 m with nothing outside it, so from a raised camera it showed as a smooth pale mound under the peristyle.
After
A drum wall and two cornice bands enclose it, so the dome now sits on a masonry drum as it does in the building.

What did not change

  • The walkable footprint, milestone order, police lines, collisions and every outcome stay in world.js, untouched.
  • No new dependency, configuration key or endpoint. No image downloads: every new surface is painted to a canvas at load.
  • All new detail follows the existing graphics ladder; small detail disappears with the extras rung as before.
  • No likenesses: paintings, statues and the canopy are abstract compositions, as the PRD requires.
