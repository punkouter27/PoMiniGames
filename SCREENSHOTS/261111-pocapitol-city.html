What was added

  • West: the Washington Monument on its knoll, with the National Gallery, Natural History (domed) and Air and Space masses along the Mall axis.
  • North: Union Station — a 120 m colonnade with a tall central pavilion and attic — then three NoMa blocks.
  • East: a terrace of fifteen Capitol Hill row houses, Eastern Market with its lantern and hipped roof, and Lincoln Park's raised green.
  • South: three L'Enfant Plaza slabs, the Marine Barracks wall and barrack, and the Wharf/Navy Yard blocks.
  • Closer in: three House and three Senate office buildings flanking the west front, plus the Botanic Garden conservatory.
  • The grounds plane grew to 420 m by 520 m, the camera far plane to 420 m, and the haze thinned from 0.0062 to 0.0048 so the ring reads as silhouettes rather than disappearing.

Cost

  • Draw calls on the aerial view fell from about 5,800 to about 1,790 at the same time as all of this was added: the campus now batches identical blocks, windows and tree branches into instanced meshes.
  • Everything is procedural boxes, cylinders, spheres and extrusions on the existing canvas-painted materials. No image downloads, no new dependency, no configuration.
  • Simulation, collisions and objectives are untouched: world.js was not modified.
